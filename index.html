Method and honesty

  • Edition. Counts follow the Hafs 'an 'Asim text: 114 surahs, 6,236 verses. Other recitations and counting rules can differ.
  • Exact forms. A reported count usually refers to one specific written form of a word. State the rule beside every number.
  • Status badges. "Verified" means checked here. "Reported" means widely cited and should be recounted from your own text before it is stated as fact.
  • Interpretation. Ring structures, scientific readings and applied history are scholarly proposals. Readers and scholars differ, and the text stands on its own.
  • Left out. Claims scholars widely reject, such as the "code 19" system, are not included.
